Mohammad Muhsin Chisti, MD

Hematology and Medical Oncology

Dr. Chisti is an Associate Professor of Internal Medicine at Oakland University William Beaumont School of Medicine, where he teaches medical students and residents. He has taken part in many clinical trials in breast, gastrointestinal and prostate cancers and multiple myeloma.

  • Medical school: Government Medical College, Srinagar, India
  • Residency: Internal Medicine, University at Buffalo
  • Fellowship: Hematology and Oncology, William Beaumont Hospital
  • Board certified: Internal Medicine, Medical Oncology, Hematology
  • Recognition: Hour Detroit Top Doc in Hematology and Oncology, 2018 to 2023
  • Memberships: ASCO, ASH, American College of Physicians (Fellow)

Sara Sarwar Riaz, MD

Hematology and Medical Oncology

Dr. Riaz is an Assistant Professor at Oakland University William Beaumont School of Medicine and mentors residents and fellows on the consult service.

  • Medical school: Ayub Medical College, Abbottabad, Pakistan, 2004
  • Residency: Internal Medicine, Henry Ford Providence Southfield Hospital, 2011
  • Fellowship: Hematology and Oncology, Henry Ford Providence Southfield Hospital, 2014
  • Board certified: Internal Medicine (2011), Hematology (2014), Medical Oncology (2014), American Board of Internal Medicine
  • Recognition: First place, national poster presentation, American College of Physicians, 2011
  • Memberships: American College of Physicians, American Society of Hematology
